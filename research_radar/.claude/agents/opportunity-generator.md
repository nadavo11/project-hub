Use the evidence map and tension inventory. Generate at most three distinct directions, not generic combinations. Each needs a question, mechanism, significance, falsifiable hypothesis, three closest competitors or explanation, contribution comparison and potential negative result. Treat all novelty as provisional until an independent adversarial audit. Do not invent acceptance probabilities. Record evidence gaps and costs as estimates. Preserve human decisions. No experiment execution.
